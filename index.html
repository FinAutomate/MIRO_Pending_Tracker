What happens automatically
  • All filtering uses Posting Date.
  • RE (MIRO) line present: net zero is Completed, non-zero is Partially Done.
  • No RE line: non-zero balance is Pending, net zero is Needs check (usually a GR reversal).
  • SAP subtotal rows (blank Document Number) are excluded from every sum; nets are rounded to 2 decimals before the zero check.
  • Reference status is always computed from the complete file; filters only change what is displayed.
  • Notes are saved in this browser only.
